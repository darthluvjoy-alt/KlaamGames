Privacy Policy for Eclipse Relics

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Eclipse Relics (the "Game") is developed and operated by Klaam Games ("we", "us", "our"). Klaam Games is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. This policy explains what data the Game uses, why it is used, who receives it, and the choices and rights available to you.

Gameplay analytics and marketing attribution are separate choices. From version 1.0.1, the Game sends first-party gameplay and technical analytics on the basis of our legitimate interests in fixing bugs and improving the Game. You can turn this off at any time under Settings → Gameplay data; turning it off does not limit gameplay. Install attribution and marketing measurement are off by default and start only if you separately choose to enable them in the Game; you can change that choice later under Settings → Ad measurement. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency ("ATT") choice is requested first and is separate from the Game's Tenjin/Meta measurement choice. Builds released before version 1.0.1 ask for permission in-game before any optional analytics starts.

How to delete your account and data

In the Game, open Settings → Delete Account and confirm the request. The Game sends the request to our server and removes local data only after the server confirms that the PlayFab player account has been permanently deleted.

If you cannot open the Game, email darthluvjoy@hotmail.com. Include your in-game display name and, if available, your player ID so we can identify the correct account. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Deletion removes the player account, saved progress, inventory, currencies, leaderboard and event entries, guild membership and applications, player-linked chat and activity entries, and other player-linked backend data. If other members remain, a guild itself, including its name and settings, may remain and leadership may be reassigned. Information that must be retained for legal, transaction, fraud-prevention, or security purposes may be kept only for the required period. Requests sent to analytics and attribution providers are queued after the PlayFab deletion is confirmed and are normally completed within 30 days.

Analytics, attribution, and your choices

First-party gameplay analytics. From version 1.0.1, the Game sends pseudonymous gameplay and technical analytics to PlayFab analytics by default, based on our legitimate interests in understanding how the Game is played, finding and fixing bugs, crashes, and freezes, and improving and balancing the Game. This data is used only by us, is tied to your pseudonymous player ID rather than your name, and is never used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. You can object and turn it off at any time under Settings → Gameplay data: collection stops immediately, queued analytics events are cleared, and gameplay is not limited in any way. Builds released before version 1.0.1 ask for permission in-game before enabling optional analytics.

Install attribution and marketing measurement. Attribution is off by default, is controlled by the separate Ad measurement choice, and never inherits the Gameplay data setting. On a supported Android build, separately consented Tenjin measurement may use available advertising or App Set identifiers, install information, selected conversion events, and verified-purchase data. Eclipse Relics does not display third-party ads, and we do not sell personal data.

iOS and ATT. After the tutorial, the Game requests Apple's native ATT choice first and then presents the Game's separate marketing-attribution choice. If you decline the Game's choice, Tenjin is not initialized or connected regardless of your ATT choice. If you enable Ad measurement and ATT is authorized, Tenjin may receive IDFA and IDFV, app, device and network context, named events such as tutorial completion, first summon, first battle win and campaign stage clear, and product, price, currency, transaction and StoreKit proof for verified purchases. Tenjin may send corresponding install, event, and purchase-measurement callbacks to Meta; the Game does not send StoreKit proof directly to Meta. If ATT is denied or restricted but you enable Ad measurement, the Game sends no IDFA, named custom event, transaction ID, or purchase proof to Tenjin. Tenjin may process IDFV and app, device or network data for this app's install only where iOS permits, while the Game updates Apple's SKAdNetwork conversion value for aggregate install, tutorial, summon, stage-clear, and purchase measurement. Meta receives only aggregate SKAdNetwork measurement. We do not link the PlayFab player ID to Tenjin.

The Gameplay data and Ad measurement controls are independent. Turning Ad measurement off calls Tenjin's opt-out function; while opted out, no new attribution events are routed by the Game and Tenjin states that its SDK sends no API requests or processes events. This does not change Apple's ATT setting, which you can manage in iOS Settings. On Android, you may reset or limit the advertising identifier in the device's privacy settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before it. You may use Delete Account or contact us to request deletion of previously processed data. Core PlayFab data needed for sign-in, saved progress, multiplayer, purchases, security, and account deletion continues to be processed so the Game can work.

Service providers and recipients

  • Microsoft Azure PlayFab: guest authentication, optional recovery-email and password sign-in, cloud saves, game services, CloudScript, multiplayer data, leaderboards, and first-party gameplay analytics (on by default from version 1.0.1; you can turn it off under Gameplay data in Settings).
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit: payment processing, purchase confirmation, and store receipt handling on iOS. Apple handles payment credentials under its own privacy terms.
  • Google Play Billing: payment processing and purchase confirmation on Android. Google handles payment credentials under its own privacy terms.
  • Tenjin: install attribution and marketing measurement on supported Android and iOS builds, only after the separate Ad measurement choice. Depending on platform permissions, Tenjin may process device and app information, IDFV, IDFA or Android advertising identifiers, campaign information, selected conversion events, and verified-purchase information. See Tenjin's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta: may receive campaign-measurement callbacks through Tenjin. On iOS with ATT authorization, these may include install, selected event, and purchase-measurement callbacks; when ATT is denied or restricted, Meta receives only aggregate SKAdNetwork measurement. The Game does not include the Meta SDK. Meta processes measurement data under its Business Tools Terms and Privacy Policy.

Providers process data under their agreements and privacy terms. Some providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, using the transfer safeguards available under applicable data-protection law.

Legal bases

Where the European Economic Area or United Kingdom data-protection rules apply, we process core account, gameplay, multiplayer, and purchase data because it is necessary to provide the Game you request. We process security and fraud-prevention data based on our legitimate interests in protecting players and the service, and we retain information where required by law. First-party gameplay and technical analytics are processed based on our legitimate interests in understanding, securing, and improving the Game; you can object at any time using the Gameplay data setting, and in builds released before version 1.0.1 this processing is based on consent instead. Install attribution and marketing measurement are based on the separate consent you give through the Ad measurement choice. On iOS, Apple's ATT authorization is a separate system permission required before we access IDFA or permit install-linked cross-company tracking; ATT authorization alone never enables Tenjin, and declining or withdrawing the Game's Ad measurement choice overrides it.

Retention

Core game data is retained while the account remains active and until it is deleted. Chat, reports, and guild activity may be kept in limited rolling histories or for as long as reasonably needed for the feature, moderation, security, or support. Purchase, fraud-prevention, and legal records may be retained for the period required by applicable law or a legitimate security need. The local Ad measurement choice remains until you change it or clear the Game's local data. If Tenjin has previously been initialized, its installation identifier is cached locally after opt-out only so a later deletion request can be routed; it is removed after verified account deletion or local-data reset. Tenjin's current Data Processing Addendum states that attribution data is retained for between 30 days and 24 months depending on customer and partner contractual requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymized unless law requires otherwise. Meta's Business Tools Terms permit Event Data to be retained for a maximum of two years, while Meta may preserve information longer where required for legal, security, or integrity purposes. An account-deletion request queues the identifiers needed for applicable provider-side deletion.

Security

Data sent between the Game and its online services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access is limited to systems and people that need it for operation, support, security, or legal compliance. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent. You can object to first-party analytics under Settings → Gameplay data and withdraw marketing-attribution consent under Settings → Ad measurement. On iOS, you can separately change ATT permission in the device's Settings. Withdrawal stops future processing as described above but does not itself delete data already processed; use Delete Account or contact us for deletion. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. We may need enough information to verify that the request concerns your account.

Children

Eclipse Relics is intended for players aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and revision date will remain available on this page. Material changes will be communicated where required.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, deletion requests, and requests concerning Tenjin or Meta attribution data can be sent to Klaam Games at darthluvjoy@hotmail.com. Include your in-game display name and player ID if available, but do not send passwords, StoreKit receipts, transaction identifiers, advertising identifiers, or payment-card details.
